What We Build

Custom Cabinetry & Furniture Services

We specialize in creating custom cabinets and storage solutions for your home or office. Our team of experienced craftsmen works closely with you to assess your needs, measure your space, and design a solution that meets your specific requirements — using solid wood, MDF, plywood, laminates, metal, glass or stone, depending on the project.

01

Custom-Made Kitchens

Eco WoodWork & Design creates custom-made kitchen cabinets designed to meet your unique style and functional needs. Our team of experienced craftsmen uses only high-quality materials — solid wood, MDF, metal, and laminates — to create durable cabinets built to last, tailored to the exact dimensions and layout of your kitchen. You choose from a wide range of finishes, colors, and hardware options to match your preferred style.

02

Custom-Made Vanities

We offer custom-made vanities designed to meet the specific needs and dimensions of your bathroom space. Our skilled professionals work closely with you to create a functional, stylish storage solution using solid wood, plywood, or MDF — with drawers, shelves, built-in organizers, and unique finishes such as decorative trim or custom hardware to enhance the overall look of your bathroom.

03

Custom-Made Bars

A custom-made bar provides a unique, personalized entertainment space for your home or business. Bars can be designed to fit around existing architectural features, incorporate custom lighting, mirrors, or decorative trim work, and include functional elements such as refrigerators, wine racks, and ice makers — built from wood, metal, glass, or stone.

04

Custom-Made Closets

Transform your closet into a personalized storage solution that perfectly fits your needs and preferences. Our custom-made closets typically combine shelves, rods, and drawers that can be adjusted in size and number to accommodate all types of clothing, shoes, and accessories — built from solid wood, MDF, metal, or laminates in a traditional, modern, or rustic style.

05

Custom Garage Cabinets

Custom garage cabinets are built to perfectly fit the specific needs and dimensions of your garage, from wood, metal, or a combination of both — sturdy enough for everyday garage use. They can be designed around obstacles like support poles or HVAC units, and take advantage of unused wall space or high ceilings to store tools, sports equipment, and gardening supplies.

06

Custom Playroom Cabinets

Our custom playroom cabinets are designed specifically to meet your family's storage needs while adding style and personality to the space. Whether you need shelves, drawers, or a combination of both, we build a customized solution for storing toys, books, and games — in the materials, finishes, and style that match your home.

07

Custom Game Room Cabinets

Just like our playroom and living-space built-ins, custom game room cabinets are designed around how you use the room — storage for games, equipment, and displays, built from solid wood, MDF, plywood, laminates, metal, glass, or stone to match your style.

08

Custom Office Cabinets

Custom office cabinets and desks are designed and built around your workspace — filing storage, shelving, and desks sized to fit the room, in finishes that match the rest of your home or business.

09

Custom Wall Units

From media walls to built-in libraries, our custom wall units are designed to make full use of your wall space — combining open shelving, closed storage, and display niches built to the exact dimensions of the room.

10

Custom Walking Pantries

A custom walking pantry turns unused space into organized, accessible storage — shelving, counters, and cabinetry designed around how you cook and stock your kitchen.

11

Custom Furniture

Add a unique touch to your home with custom, hand-built furniture. Our focus on quality and personalization gives you a one-of-a-kind piece — built to your vision, from the material to the finish.

12

Wood Remodeling

Revamp your existing furniture with our wood remodeling services, personalized to fit your style and needs — bringing new life to a dining table, chair, or living-room set with quality and attention to detail.

13

Old Furniture Remodeling

Our passion for wood and remodeling art brings new life to old furniture. We work with a variety of woods and techniques to give worn pieces a fresh look, with consultation to help you make informed decisions that fit your style and budget.

How We Work

Our process, on every project

Whatever you're building — a kitchen, a closet, or a one-of-a-kind piece of furniture — we follow the same seven steps to get it right.

1

Assess Needs

Determine what you'll be storing and how you'll use the space.

2

Measure Space

Measure the space to determine the exact size and shape to build.

3

Choose Materials

Decide on materials based on budget, durability, and maintenance.

4

Plan the Design

We create a design considering placement, style, and finish.

5

Build

Our experienced team hand-builds your piece from the materials selected.

6

Install

We install securely, leveled and fastened, on site.

7

Finishing Touches

Hardware, trim, and details that complete the look.
