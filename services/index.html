What we build

Custom work.
Every room.

We specialize in custom cabinets and storage solutions for your home or office. Our experienced craftsmen work closely with you to assess your needs, measure your space and design a solution built specifically for it — using solid wood, MDF, plywood, laminates, metal, glass or stone, depending on the project.

Custom Kitchens

Eco Woodwork & Design creates custom-made kitchen cabinets designed to meet your unique style and functional needs. Our craftsmen use high-quality materials — solid wood, MDF, metal and laminates — to create durable cabinets tailored to the exact dimensions and layout of your kitchen, with a wide range of finishes, colors and hardware.

  • Custom-Made Kitchens

    Cabinetry designed and built around how you cook and live.

  • Custom Walking Pantries

    Unused space turned into organized, accessible storage — shelving, counters and cabinetry designed around how you stock your kitchen.

How we work

Our process, on every project.

Whatever you're building — a kitchen, a closet or a one-of-a-kind piece — we follow the same steps to get it right.

  2. 01

    Consultation

    We assess what you need to store, how you'll use the space, and measure it precisely.

  3. 02

    Design

    We plan placement, style and finish, and choose materials for budget, durability and maintenance.

  4. 03

    Craft

    Our experienced team builds your piece from the materials selected.

  5. 04

    Installation

    Installed securely — leveled and fastened on site.

  6. 05

    Transformation

    Hardware, trim and details that complete the look.
